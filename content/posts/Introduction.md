## About me

Recently graduated from the [HEIG-VD](https://heig-vd.ch) with a Bachelor in Software Engineering.

I am driven by curiosity and have a deep passion for the myriad facets of *video game developement*.
Be it the technical challenges or the UX ones.

My Bachelor's thesis focused on state of the art performance for 3D real time applications, namely, video games.
The work was written in French and has earned [the highest possible grading](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_grading_in_Switzerland) (6/6).
It can be found publicly [On my GitHub](https://github.com/GuggisbergSimon/HEIG-VD-TB).

Aside from computers I have an interest for Japan. Not only about its modern pop culture impact, but also its tradition, history, and martial arts.
I've obtained a black belt in the [Bodaïshinkan Ryu](https://www.reighikan-dojo.ch/) school, for japanese weapons.

I also hike in the beautiful Switzerland mountains, when time and mood strikes.
